Stock Valuation Mechanics with a Perpetuity: RSM 1631

Forecast the first T cash flows individually. From the Tth cash flow onward, assume a perpetuity growing at rate g forever. The fair value V0 is the present value of all cash flows, discounted at r.

The perpetuity is valued one period before its first payment: PVT−1 = CFT / (r − g), then discounted back to today by (1 + r)T−1. The first T − 1 cash flows are each discounted individually. Requires r > g.
